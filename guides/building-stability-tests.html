Building Stability Tests in Archibrix

Explore five interactive challenges available in Archibrix: Block Builder’s Free Build Mode.

From Building to Experimenting

Archibrix combines block placement puzzles with a creative Free Build Mode. Players can create structures and test them using simulated environmental and physical challenges. Instead of stopping when the building is complete, you can explore a design, try a test and adjust the blocks for another attempt.

Five Available Tests

Flood

Experiment with a rising-water scenario and observe the effects in the game. Try different building layouts and compare the results.

Rain

Introduce rainy weather to your structure. Explore different constructions under the game's rain conditions.

Wind

Use the wind test to challenge your design. Change shapes and arrangements to explore the response.

Earthquake

Run a simulated earthquake to see how the structure behaves during in-game ground movement.

Weight

Experiment with additional weight and discover how the game responds to changes in the load on your building.

Ideas for Free Build Experiments

Build two different towers and compare their behavior under wind. Design a small house and test it in rain and flood scenarios. Try different block arrangements before applying weight or earthquake tests. Repeat a test after changing one part of your structure to compare the outcomes.

Is This a Real Engineering Simulator?

No. Archibrix is a creative mobile game. Its tests are interactive simulations for entertainment and experimentation and should not be used to make real-world engineering or safety decisions.
